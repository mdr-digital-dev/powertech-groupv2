PowerTech Partners with Huntress to Bring 24/7 Log Monitoring to Chicago Businesses

By PowerTech Group of Chicago  ·  October 2026  ·  6 min read

Every server, firewall, laptop and login on your network keeps a record of what happens on it. Those records, called logs, are often the first place an attack shows up. A password tried a hundred times overnight. An admin account created at 3 a.m. A firewall rule changed with no explanation.

The problem is that almost nobody is reading them. Most small and mid-sized businesses collect logs because they're supposed to, then let them sit until something goes wrong. By that point, the attacker has usually been inside for weeks.

That's why PowerTech Group of Chicago has partnered with Huntress to offer 24/7 managed log monitoring to our clients.

What This Partnership Means for You

Huntress is a cybersecurity company built for businesses that don't have a full security team in-house. Its Managed SIEM provides real-time detection — logs are collected from across your environment and watched around the clock by a 24/7 human SOC, where analysts investigate suspicious activity rather than an alert that lands in an inbox nobody checks. Huntress reports a mean time to respond of 8 minutes from alert generation to incident report or closure, with 85% of alerts resolved within 15 minutes and 97% within 30.

With PowerTech managing the setup and Huntress providing round-the-clock monitoring, you get:

  • 24/7 eyes on your logs, including nights, weekends and holidays, when most attacks are launched
  • Logs from across your environment in one place: servers, workstations, firewalls, VPNs, and Microsoft 365 sign-ins
  • Less noise. Routine events are filtered out so real threats aren't buried under thousands of harmless entries.
  • Human-led investigation. Suspicious activity is triaged by security analysts, not just flagged by software.
  • One local point of contact. PowerTech handles deployment, tuning and follow-up, so you're never on your own when something needs attention.

Huntress SOC response times: Mean time to respond is 8 minutes from alert generation to incident report or closure. 85% of alerts are resolved within 15 minutes and 97% within 30 minutes — backed by human analysts, not just automated rules.

Why Logs Matter for PCI DSS Compliance

If your business accepts credit or debit cards, you're required to follow the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS). Requirement 10 of the standard is dedicated entirely to logging and monitoring, and it's one of the areas where businesses most often fall short.

Under PCI DSS v4.0.1, businesses are expected to:

  • Log the right events, including access to cardholder data, administrative actions, failed login attempts, and changes to accounts and permissions (Requirement 10.2)
  • Review security logs at least once a day for systems that store, process or protect cardholder data (Requirement 10.4.1)
  • Use automated mechanisms to perform those log reviews. This became mandatory on March 31, 2025 (Requirement 10.4.1.1).
  • Keep at least 12 months of log history, with the most recent three months immediately available for analysis (Requirement 10.5.1)
  • Detect and respond when critical security controls fail, including logging itself (Requirement 10.7.2)

Daily log review is nearly impossible to do by hand. Most businesses simply don't have someone who can sit down every morning and sort through thousands of entries from every system on the network. Automated, continuously monitored logging is the practical way to meet these requirements, and it's exactly what this service provides.

A note on compliance: PCI DSS compliance covers your whole environment and is a shared responsibility. Managed log monitoring addresses a key part of it, Requirement 10, and PowerTech can help you assess the rest.

Who Needs 24/7 Log Monitoring?

This service is a strong fit for any Chicago-area business that:

  • Accepts card payments in-store, online or over the phone, especially retailers, grocery stores, restaurants and service businesses
  • Has gone through a PCI assessment and been told logging and monitoring need work
  • Doesn't have a dedicated security team but still needs enterprise-level protection
  • Handles sensitive customer or employee data and wants to know right away if something looks wrong

Security That Works While You Sleep

Attackers don't keep business hours. Ransomware groups routinely strike on Friday nights and holiday weekends, when they know fewer people are watching. When an incident starts, the difference between catching it in minutes and catching it on Monday morning can mean the difference between a quick fix and a business-wide shutdown. With Huntress's SOC averaging an 8-minute mean time to respond — and resolving 97% of alerts within 30 minutes — that window is as short as it gets.

With 24/7 monitoring in place, suspicious activity gets investigated when it happens, not when someone finally gets around to checking.
