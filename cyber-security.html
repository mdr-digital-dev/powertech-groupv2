What You Get

What's Included

Every layer of protection your business needs — deployed and monitored by our team.

Endpoint Detection & Response (EDR)

Huntress-powered EDR on every device — detecting malware, ransomware, and advanced threats using behavioral analysis, not just known signatures.

Next-Gen Firewall Management

SonicWall and Cisco Meraki firewalls configured with strict traffic rules, geo-blocking, and intrusion prevention — actively managed and updated by our team.

Email Security & Anti-Phishing

Advanced email filtering blocks phishing, malware attachments, and business email compromise (BEC) before they reach your team's inbox.

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA enforced across email, VPN, and remote access — so a stolen password alone can't open the door to your systems.

24/7 Security Monitoring & Alerting

Continuous monitoring of your network, endpoints, and logs. Our Huntress-backed SOC investigates alerts around the clock and escalates real threats immediately.

Dark Web Monitoring

We scan criminal forums and breach databases for your company's credentials. If your data surfaces, you're alerted immediately so you can act before an attacker does.

Security Awareness Training

Simulated phishing campaigns and training modules that turn your employees into an active line of defense — not your biggest vulnerability.

Network Segmentation & Access Control

VLANs that isolate your most sensitive systems — keeping POS terminals, cameras, and guest Wi-Fi off your primary business network.

1

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Layered security means protecting your business at multiple levels simultaneously — the firewall that sits at your network perimeter, the endpoint detection software running on each device, the email filter that blocks phishing before it reaches a user, and the monitoring system watching for unusual behavior 24/7. No single tool is enough; the layers work together to catch what any one layer might miss.
Traditional antivirus looks for known malware signatures. Modern endpoint detection and response (EDR) tools, like Huntress, use behavioral analysis to detect threats that have never been seen before — including fileless attacks, living-off-the-land techniques, and ransomware in its early stages. Antivirus is a component, not a complete solution.
Dark web monitoring scans criminal marketplaces and data-breach repositories for your company's email addresses and credentials. If a password tied to your business shows up in a known breach, you're alerted immediately so you can change credentials before an attacker can use them.
Yes. Phishing and social engineering are behind the majority of successful breaches. Security awareness training teaches your team to recognize suspicious emails, links, and requests — turning your staff from a vulnerability into a line of defense.
