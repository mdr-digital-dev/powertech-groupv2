Why MSP?

Reactive IT vs. PowerTech MSP

Most businesses call an IT person when something breaks. That's expensive, unpredictable, and reactive. Here's what changes when you have a real MSP.

Reactive IT (Break/Fix)

  • Pay per incident — surprise bills every time
  • No monitoring — you notice the problem first
  • Hours or days to get someone on-site
  • No cybersecurity unless you ask and pay extra
  • Multiple vendors, no single point of accountability
  • No reporting — zero visibility into your IT health

PowerTech MSP

  • Flat monthly rate — no surprise bills
  • 24/7 monitoring — we catch it before you do
  • Remote + on-site helpdesk with fast response
  • Endpoint protection & threat detection included
  • One team for IT, cybersecurity & physical security
  • Monthly reports — full visibility into your environment
