What You Get

What's Included in Managed IT

Every PowerTech managed client gets a consistent, proactive service — not just break-fix support when something goes wrong.

24/7 Network & Device Monitoring

Continuous monitoring of your servers, workstations, and network devices — alerts and response before issues escalate.

Security Patching & Updates

Automated deployment of OS and software patches, so your systems stay current and protected without manual effort.

Helpdesk Support (Remote & On-Site)

Real people, fast response — handling tickets, troubleshooting issues, and providing on-site visits when needed.

Firewall & Network Management

Ongoing management of your firewall, VPN, switches, and network configuration to keep everything secure and performing.

Regular Health Reports & Check-Ins

Scheduled reviews with system health reporting so you always know the state of your IT and can plan ahead.

IT Planning & Budgeting Guidance

We help you plan equipment refreshes and IT investments ahead of time — no surprise costs or reactive replacements.

Our Process

How Managed IT Works

1

Assessment

We review your devices, network, and infrastructure to understand what you have, find any gaps, and document your environment.

2

Onboarding

We deploy monitoring tools, standardize configurations, and set up remote management — with minimal disruption to your team.

3

Ongoing Management

We monitor 24/7, apply patches automatically, handle helpdesk tickets, and keep your systems running and secure.

4

Regular Reviews

We meet with you to review system health, discuss upcoming needs, and plan upgrades so your IT stays ahead of your business.
