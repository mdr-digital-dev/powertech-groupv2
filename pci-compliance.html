What You Get

What's Included

From initial assessment through ongoing annual reviews — we manage the technical requirements so you stay compliant.

Network Segmentation & VLAN Design

We isolate your cardholder data environment (CDE) from the rest of your network using VLANs and firewall rules — reducing your PCI scope and your exposure.

Firewall Configuration & Review

Active firewall rules, port restrictions, and traffic policies that satisfy PCI Requirement 1 — with documentation your assessor can review.

24/7 Log Monitoring (Huntress)

Continuous monitoring and retention of logs from your CDE, satisfying PCI Requirement 10. Huntress-backed SOC reviews alerts around the clock and escalates real threats immediately.

Vulnerability Scanning

Internal and external vulnerability scans to identify weaknesses in your environment — required quarterly under PCI DSS Requirement 11.

Documentation & Network Diagrams

Complete network diagrams, data flow documentation, and written policies that assessors and QSAs need to verify your environment.

SAQ Guidance & Completion Support

We walk you through the Self-Assessment Questionnaire with plain-language explanations of each requirement — and help you document your answers accurately.

Access Control & MFA Enforcement

Least-privilege access policies, unique user accounts, and multi-factor authentication enforced across all systems that touch payment data.

Annual Compliance Reviews

PCI DSS requirements change as your business evolves. We schedule annual reviews to keep your controls, documentation, and configurations current.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

PCI DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) applies to any business that accepts, processes, stores, or transmits credit card data. If your business takes credit cards — whether in person or online — you're required to comply. The specific requirements depend on your transaction volume and how cards are processed.
Network segmentation means isolating the systems that touch cardholder data (like POS terminals) from the rest of your network — your office computers, security cameras, and guest Wi-Fi. This is done using VLANs and firewall rules. Proper segmentation dramatically reduces the scope of your PCI assessment and limits the blast radius if something is compromised.
Yes. PCI DSS Requirement 10 mandates that you monitor and retain logs of all access to your cardholder data environment. PowerTech now offers 24/7 log monitoring through Huntress, which satisfies this requirement with a SOC-backed service that reviews logs continuously and alerts your team to any suspicious activity.
A Self-Assessment Questionnaire (SAQ) is a compliance checklist that most small and mid-sized merchants complete on their own. A QSA (Qualified Security Assessor) is a certified auditor required for larger merchants or those that have experienced a breach. PowerTech guides businesses through SAQ completion and can help determine which SAQ type applies to your environment.
For most small to mid-sized businesses, achieving compliance typically takes a few weeks once work begins — including assessment, remediation, and documentation. The timeline depends on how close your current environment is to meeting requirements. We'll give you a realistic picture after the initial assessment.
