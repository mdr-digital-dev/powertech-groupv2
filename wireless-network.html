IT Services

Business Wi-Fi & Network Installation

Enterprise wireless networks and structured cabling, designed and managed using Cisco Meraki — so your connections stay fast and reliable.

Home/ IT Services/ Wireless Network

PowerTech designs, installs, and manages enterprise Wi-Fi and cabling for commercial facilities using Cisco Meraki. Whether you're setting up a new office or upgrading an older network, we make sure connections stay fast and reliable across your entire building or campus.

What You Get

What's Included

From site survey to ongoing management — we handle the full scope of your network infrastructure.

Site Survey & Network Design

We walk your facility to map coverage, assess cabling, and design a system that fits your space and usage requirements.

Cisco Meraki Access Point Installation

Enterprise-grade access points, professionally mounted and configured for maximum coverage and performance.

Structured Cabling & Patch Panels

Proper Cat6/Cat6A cabling, termination, labeling, and patch panel installation for a clean, organized infrastructure.

VLAN Setup & Network Segmentation

Separate networks for staff, guests, cameras, and other devices — keeping your data secure and your bandwidth organized.

Cloud-Managed Network Dashboard

All your network devices managed through Cisco Meraki's cloud platform — visible and controllable from anywhere.

Ongoing Management & Troubleshooting

Remote firmware updates, configuration changes, and troubleshooting — available as part of your managed IT plan.

Our Process

How It Works

1

Site Survey

We walk your facility to map coverage requirements, assess existing cabling, and identify the right placement and quantity of access points.

2

Design & Quote

We provide a network design and equipment list based on your usage needs and building layout — before any work starts.

3

Installation

We install access points, run cabling, configure the network, and test everything — with minimal disruption to your operation.

4

Ongoing Management

Your network is monitored and managed through Cisco Meraki's cloud platform — firmware updates, configuration changes, and troubleshooting handled remotely.
